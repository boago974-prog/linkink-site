About LINKINK

One artist.
Every Sunday.

LINKINK is a weekly illustration magazine built around a single idea: give one artist the whole week, and let the work breathe.

01

The idea

Most magazines cover a scene. We'd rather sit with one person and find out how the work actually happens.

Every Sunday we publish one long-form feature on one working illustrator: a proper interview, a gallery of pages from their sketchbook, and a speedpaint video of the work being made. No round-ups, no "ten illustrators to watch", no digest.

We started LINKINK because the most interesting part of illustration is almost never the finished image. It's the eleventh attempt, the page that got abandoned, the pencil that runs out mid-line. That's what we publish — and it's why every artist gets a week rather than a paragraph.

— The LINKINK editors

How a week works

Same shape, every Sunday.

Step 01

One artist gets the whole week

A long-form spotlight, an eight-page gallery, and a speedpaint video — all published on Sunday morning.

Step 02

Depth over breadth

No lists, no digests, no "rising stars". One proper conversation rather than twenty short ones.

Step 03

Then it stays up

Every feature joins the archive permanently and stays free to read. Nothing is ever paywalled.

03

Apply to be featured

We read every application ourselves.

We're always looking for the next artist of the week. No agent, no pitch deck, no follower count — just the work and a bit about how you make it.

What we look for

  • A practice you can talk about, not just a portfolio
  • Work that shows process — sketchbooks, drafts, failures
  • A voice that holds up over a long interview
  • Illustration in any form: editorial, books, print, motion, zines
  • Artists at any stage — first feature or fiftieth

What to send

The form asks for the basics. If you'd rather email, send the same five things to apply@linkink.co.bw and we'll reply the same way.

We reply to every application within two weeks, whether or not it's a yes. If it's a no, it isn't a permanent one — we often come back to applications months later.

04

Questions

The things we're asked most.

01 Do I need an agent or representation?

No. Over half the artists we've featured applied directly, and a good number had never been interviewed before. If your work is ready, that's the only requirement.

02 Do you pay?

Yes. Every featured artist receives a flat fee of $250 for the interview, gallery and video, plus a share of any print sales that come from the feature.

03 Is it only illustration?

Mostly — but we've featured printmakers, muralists and motion designers. If it's drawn, it counts. If you're not sure whether your practice fits, apply anyway and let us decide.

04 How long does a feature take?

About three weeks from a yes to publication: one long interview, one studio visit (or a video call if you're far away), and a set of images and video from your archive. We do the editing and layout.

05 Can I apply again?

Yes, after six months. A number of artists have come back with new work, and we're always glad to see it.

06 What if I'm not selected?

We keep your application on file and often come back to it later. No rejection is permanent. You're welcome to apply again whenever you have new work to show.
